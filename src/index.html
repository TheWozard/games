Games

  • SudokuFill the grid so every row, column and box has 1–9.
  • Killer SudokuSudoku with no clues, just cages: each one adds up to its sum, with no repeats.
  • MinesClear the field using the counts of hidden mines next to each cell. No guessing needed.
  • BridgesLink the islands with bridges so each has its number, without crossings, all connected.
  • GradientSwap tiles until the colours flow smoothly from corner to corner.
  • UntangleDrag the dots until none of the lines cross. No clock, no rush.
  • MahjongClear the stacked tiles by matching free pairs. Every deal can be cleared.
