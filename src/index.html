  • SudokuClassic, Killer or Jigsaw: fill the grid so every row, column and box has 1–9.

    Sudoku

    Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box holds 1–9 once.

    Rules

    • Some numbers are given and can’t be changed.
    • A number repeated in a row, column or box turns red.

    How to Play

    • Pick a tool and a number under the board, then tap cells.
    • Pencil: writes the number, replacing what’s in the cell. Tap a cell that already holds it to clear it.
    • Notebook: adds the number as a small note, in empty cells or ones with notes. Drag to note several; start on a cell that has the note to take it away instead.
    • Eraser: clears a cell. Drag to clear several.
    • Writing a number takes it out of the notes in its row, column and box. Cells with the picked number are shaded.

    Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

  • ShadingNonogram, Star Battle, Nurikabe, Hitori and Light Up: decide which cells to fill.

    Shading

    Shade the cells to match the numbers around the grid.

    Rules

    • The numbers beside each row and above each column are the lengths of its runs of shaded cells, in order.
    • Runs are split by at least one empty cell.
    • A line’s numbers dim when the line matches them.

    How to Play

    • The circle at the bottom right shows what a tap places: a shade (or a color), or an X for a cell you know is empty. Tap the circle to switch.
    • Tap a cell to place it, and again to clear it. With a mouse, right-click places an X.
    • Drag along a row or column to do the same to each cell you pass.
    • On a narrow screen a big grid slides sideways: drag anywhere off the cells.

    Optional Rules

    • Colors: Cells come in colors, shown on the numbers. Runs of the same color need a gap; different colors may touch.
    • Mystery: Some numbers are hidden as ?: the run is there, but its length isn’t given.

    Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

  • MinesClear the field using the counts of hidden mines next to each cell. No guessing needed.

    Mines

    Reveal every cell that isn’t a mine.

    Rules

    • Each number counts the mines in the eight cells around it.
    • Reveal a mine and the game is over.

    How to Play

    • Tap a cell to reveal it.
    • Long-press (or right-click) to flag a mine.
    • Tap a number whose mines are all flagged to reveal the rest of the cells around it.
    • There’s no Undo: a wrong tap stands.

    Your first tap is always safe, and every board can be cleared without guessing.

Paths

  • LoopDraw one closed loop along the dots; each number says how many of its sides the loop uses.

    Loop

    Draw one closed loop along the lines between the dots.

    Rules

    • The loop can’t branch or cross itself.
    • A number in a cell is how many of its four sides the loop uses. Cells without a number can have any.
    • Numbers dim when they’re met and turn red when there are too many.

    How to Play

    • Tap a side to draw a line there.
    • Tap again to mark it ✕, a side you know the loop doesn’t use, and once more to clear it.

    Optional Rules

    • Squares: Solid squares end up on one side of the loop, hollow squares on the other.
    • Rings: The loop passes through every ringed dot.

    Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

  • BridgesLink the islands with bridges so each has its number, without crossings, all connected.

    Bridges

    Connect every island, with each island’s number of bridges.

    Rules

    • Bridges run straight across or down, with at most two between the same two islands.
    • Bridges can’t cross each other.
    • All the islands end up connected as one group.

    How to Play

    • Drag from one island to another to build a bridge; drag again for a double.
    • Swipe across a bridge to take one away.
    • Or tap the gap between two islands to cycle 0 → 1 → 2 bridges.

    Optional Rules

    • Colors: Bridges join red to red and blue to blue; plain islands join any.
    • Blocks: No bridge may pass over a block.
    • Diagonals: Bridges can also run diagonally, even between islands that touch at a corner.

    Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

  • FlowJoin matching dots with paths that never cross and fill every square.

    Flow

    Join each pair of matching dots, and fill every square.

    Rules

    • Paths run through neighboring squares, side to side.
    • Paths can’t cross or share a square.

    How to Play

    • Drag from a dot to draw its path.
    • Drag back over a path to shorten it, or start on a path to carry on from there.
    • Other colors block the way: move them first.

    Optional Rules

    • Walls: Paths can’t cross the thick walls between some squares.
    • Holes: Some squares are missing; paths go around them, and they don’t need filling.
    • Wrapping: The edges join up: a path that leaves one side comes back on the opposite side.
    • Bridges: Two paths cross on a bridge, one straight across and one straight down; it’s filled once both do.

    Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

  • UntangleDrag the dots until none of the lines cross. Every puzzle can be untangled.

    Untangle

    Move the dots until no two lines cross.

    Rules

    • Lines that cross show in red.
    • A dot resting on a line it isn’t part of counts as a crossing too, and gets a red ring.

    How to Play

    • Drag a dot to move it. The whole screen is yours to spread them out.
    • A dot dropped on another is nudged clear.

    Every puzzle can be untangled.

    Color Sort

    Sort the colors so each tube ends up full of one color, or empty.

    Rules

    • Color pours only into an empty tube or onto the same color.
    • The whole run of matching color on top pours across, as much as fits.

    How to Play

    • Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another to pour into it.
    • Some pours lead to a dead end. If you run out of moves, Undo to try another way.

    • Tall Tubes: Tubes hold more layers.

Cards & tiles

  • SolitaireKlondike or FreeCell: build each suit up from Ace to King. Every deal can be won.

    Solitaire

    Move every card to the four foundations along the top, each suit built up from Ace to King.

    Rules

    • Build the columns down in alternating colors: a red 7 on a black 8.
    • Most cards start face down. Moving a column’s last face-up card turns over the one beneath it.
    • Only a King can go into an empty column.

    How to Play

    • Tap a card to send it to its best spot. A card with nowhere to go gives a shake.
    • Tap the stock (bottom right) to draw one card or three, as picked in New Game (+). When it runs out, tap again to turn the pile over.
    • Once every card is showing, the rest go home by themselves.
    • On a narrow screen the columns slide sideways under your finger.

    Every deal can be won.

  • MahjongClear the stacked tiles by matching free pairs. Every deal can be cleared.

    Mahjong

    Clear the board by matching pairs of identical tiles.

    Rules

    • Any flower matches any other flower, and any season any other season.
    • A tile is free when nothing lies on it and its left or right side is open. Dimmed tiles aren’t free yet.
    • Easy deals forgive a careless match. On harder ones, the wrong pair early can leave its twin stuck, so look ahead.

    How to Play

    • Tap a free tile, then its match.
    • If no matching pairs are free, a Shuffle button appears to mix up the tiles that are left.
    • New Game (+) picks one layout or several; each deal takes one of them at random.

    Every deal can be cleared.
